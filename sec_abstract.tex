\begin{abstract}
Davison and McBreen associate a bicharacteristic polynomial to a
symplectic-dual pair of conical symplectic resolutions. Stable envelopes
identify suitable specializations of the equivariant cohomologies of
the two resolutions; their cohomological filtrations then induce a
bifiltration on a common vector space. We compute this polynomial for
the self-dual type $A$ Springer resolution
\[
  T^*\Fln\longrightarrow\mathcal N.
\]
We prove that
\[
  T_{X,X^!}(x,y)
  =\sum_{\lambda\vdash n}
    K_{\lambda,(1^n)}(x)K_{\lambda^T,(1^n)}(y),
\]
where $K_{\lambda,(1^n)}$ is a Kostka--Foulkes polynomial. The proof
combines the semismall Springer decomposition, Koszul duality for
category $\mathcal O$, and the two commuting Springer actions arising
from Steinberg convolution. In particular, the stable-envelope map
reverses the Springer support filtration and transposes the partition
index on its associated graded.
\end{abstract}
