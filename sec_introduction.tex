\section{Introduction}

Let $\pi:X\rightarrow X_0$ be a conical symplectic resolution with a Hamiltonian action of a torus $T$ on $X$ and $X_0$ such that $\pi$ is $T$-equivariant.
Assume that $X$ has isolated $T$-fixed points.
We have an inclusion $X^T\hookrightarrow X$ and hence the restriction map. 
\[
    \text{res}:\HH_T^\bullet(X, \mathbb{C})\to \HH^\bullet_T(X^T,\mathbb{C})\simeq \mathbb{C}^{X^T}\otimes\HH_T^\bullet(\text{pt},\mathbb{C}).
\]
Specifying to a generic cocharacter $\sigma\in \lie{t}$ gives us a cohomological filtration on $\mathbb{C}^{X^T}$, denoted by $\text{res}_\sigma$.

In the theory of symplectic duality, symplectic resolutions often come in pairs. Denoted by $\pi^!:X^!\rightarrow X_0^!$ the dual symplectic resolution. One component of the duality is that there is a bijection between fixed point sets $X^T$ and ${X^!}^{T^!}$ satisfying certain conditions.
With the same procedure as above, a choice of generic cocharacter $\eta\in \lie{t^!}$ gives us a second cohomological filtration on $\mathbb{C}^{X^T}$,
\[
    \text{res}^!_\eta:\HH_{T^!}^\bullet(X^!, \mathbb{C})_\eta\to \HH^\bullet_{T^!}({X^!}^{T^!},\mathbb{C})_\eta\simeq \mathbb{C}^{{X^!}^{T^!}}\simeq \mathbb{C}^{X^T},
\]
where the last map is given by the bijection given by symplectic duality.
One can then ask what is the two-variable Hilbert series of $\mathbb{C}^{X^T}$ given by the bifiltrations $\text{res}_\sigma$ and $\text{res}^!_\eta$.

In \cite{mcbreen2025}, the authors considered the case associated with symplectic dual pairs of hypertoric varieties. They showed that in order to get an ``interesting'' polynomial, in their case is the Tutte polynomial of the matroid associated with the symplectic dual pair, one has to use instead the stable envelope construction introduced in \cite{maulik2018quantumgroupsquantumcohomology}, in place of the above restriction maps.

In this paper, we show that the bicharacteristic polynomial associated with the self-dual Springer resolution $\tilde{\nilcone} \to \nilcone$ of type $A$ is the following:

\begin{theorem}[thm:bichar_full_flag]{}
    The bicharacteristic polynomial associated with type $A$ springer resolution is
    \[
        \sum_{\lambda \in \Partition}^{} K_{\lambda,(1)^n}(x)\cdot K_{\lambda^T,(1)^n}(y).
    \]
\end{theorem}


We start by introducing some combinatorics about Young tableaux and Kostka polynomials in \Cref{chapter_combinatorics}. 

In \Cref{chapter_symplectic_duality}, we mention the basics of symplectic duality and introduce the main definition of the bicharacteristic polynomial associated with such a dual pair. 

In \Cref{chapter_springer}, we give a survey on Springer Theory, with a focus on Type $A$.

In \Cref{chapter_bbdg}, we introduce the Beilinson-Bernstein-Deligne-Gabber decomposition theorem and its application on Springer Resolutions.

Finally, we conclude by proving \Cref{thm:bichar_full_flag} in \Cref{chapter_main_thm}.
