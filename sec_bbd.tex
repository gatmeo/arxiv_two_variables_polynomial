\subsection{The BBDG Decomposition}

The Beilinson-Bernstein-Deligne-Gabber decomposition theorem provides a topological factorization of the derived pushforward of a proper morphism into building blocks from intersection cohomology.
In the most general setting, we have the following:

\begin{theorem}{\cite{Beilinson-Bernstein-Deligne1982}}
Let $f:X \to Y$ be a proper morphism of complex algebraic varieties with $X$ smooth. Then:
\[ Rf_*\mathbb{C}_X[\dim X] \cong \bigoplus_{\alpha} IC_{\overline{Y_\alpha}}(L_\alpha)[-d_\alpha] \]
where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $\CB{Y_\alpha}$ is a stratification of $Y$ such that $f$ is topologically locally trivial over each $Y_\alpha$,
    \item $L_\alpha$ are semi-simple local systems (direct sums of of irreducible local systems) on $Y_\alpha$, %whose rank equals the number of irreducible components of $f^{-1}(y)$ for $y\in Y_\alpha$,
    \item $d_\alpha$ are even integers.
\end{itemize}
\end{theorem}

\begin{definition}{}
A proper morphism $f:X \to Y$ of irreducible algebraic varieties is \vocab{semi-small} if it satisfies:
\[ \dim X \times_Y X \leq \dim X \]
where $X \times_Y X$ is the fiber product with its natural reduced structure. Equivalently, for any $y \in Y$:
\[ 2\dim f^{-1}(y) \leq \dim X - \dim Y \]
Let $\CB{Y_\alpha}$ be a stratification compatible with $f$.
A strata $Y_\alpha$ of $Y$ is called relevant if the equality holds, i.e. $2\dim f^{-1}(y) = \text{codim}(Y_\alpha)$.
\end{definition}

One can get a nicer decomposition of if $f$ is semi-small:

\begin{theorem}[thm:bbdg_semismall]{}
    Let $f:X \to Y$ be a \emph{semi-small} proper morphism with $X$ smooth and $Y$ irreducible. Then:
    \[ Rf_*\mathbb{C}_X[\dim X] \cong \bigoplus_{\alpha} \bigoplus_{L\in \textnormal{Irr Loc}(Y_\alpha)} IC_{\overline{Y_\alpha}}(L)\otimes V_{Y_\alpha,L}  \]
    where:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item There are no shifts $[-d_\alpha]$ appearing (i.e., all $d_\alpha = 0$).
        \item $L$ are irreducible local systems on $Y_\alpha$ and $V_{Y_\alpha,L}$ are multiplicity spaces, such that the direct sum of local systems over a strata $Y_\alpha$ has fibers isomorphic to the top degree cohomology $\Htop(f^{-1}(y))$ for some $y\in Y_\alpha$.
        \item The direct sum is finite and includes only all relevant strata.
    \end{itemize}
\end{theorem}

Consider the Springer resolution $\pi: \widetilde{\mathcal{N}} \to \mathcal{N}$.
According to \Cref{sec:steinberg_variety}, $\pi$ is semi-small and $\widetilde{\mathcal{N}}$ is smooth.
By \Cref{thm:bbdg_semismall}, the summand of the decomposition theorem is labelled by pairs of strata $\OO_x$ and a irreducible local system $L$ on $\OO_x$, i.e. a representation of $\pi_1(\OO_x)$.

When $G$ is simply connected, for example $G=SL_n$, the long exact homotopy sequence of $Z_G(x)\hookrightarrow G\rightarrow \OO_x$ reduces to
\[
    1\rightarrow \pi_1(\OO_x)\rightarrow \pi_0(Z_G(x))\rightarrow 1.
\]
Hence $\pi_0(Z_G(x))\simeq \pi_1(\OO_x)$.
For $G$ that is non-simply connected, the universal cover $\tilde{G}\rightarrow G$ induces a central extension
\[
    1\rightarrow \Gamma\rightarrow \pi_1(\OO_x)\rightarrow \pi_0(Z_G(x))\rightarrow 1,
\]
where $\Gamma$ is a subgroup of the fundamental group of $G$.

However, in either case, the irreducible local system $L$ arises from the decomposition theorem are comes from $\pi_0(Z_G(x))$. And hence the pairs of strata and local systems are precisely those given in the Springer Correspondence \Cref{thm:springer_correspondence}. We have the following theorem:

\begin{theorem}[thm:bbd_springer]{\cite{Springer1978, BM83}}
Let $\pi: \widetilde{\mathcal{N}} \to \mathcal{N}$ be the springer resolution. We have
\[ R\pi_*\mathbb{C}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{N}}}[2\dim\widetilde{\mathcal{N}}] \cong \bigoplus_{\OO}\bigoplus _{\chi\in \pi_0(Z_G(x))^\wedge} IC_{\overline{\mathcal{O}}}(L_\chi) \otimes \rho_{\OO,\chi},\]
where
\begin{itemize}
    \item the sum is over nilpotent orbits $\OO$,
    \item $L_\chi$ is a local system on $\OO$ as a lift of $\chi$,
    \item and $\rho_{\OO,\chi}$ is the irreducible representaion of $W$ associated with the pair $(\OO,\chi)$ under Springer Correspondence.
\end{itemize}
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Type A Springer Resolution}

In the case of Type $A$ Springer Resolution, since the closure of the nipotent orbits are simply connected, i.e. $\pi_1(\mathcal{O}_\lambda)$ are trivial, all the local system are trivial and there is only one IC sheaf over each strata. Morever, the local systems have fibers isomorphic to $\Htop(\BB_\lambda)$. Taking the hypercohomology in \Cref{thm:bbd_springer} gives the followiing:

\begin{corollary}[crl:type_A_decomposition]{}
    Let $\pi: \widetilde{\mathcal{N}} \to \mathcal{N}$ be the springer resolution of type $A$. Then
    \[ \HH_T^\bullet(\widetilde{\mathcal{N}}) \cong \bigoplus_{\lambda\in \Partition} IH_T^\bullet(\overline{\mathcal{O}}_\lambda) \otimes \Htop_T(\BB_\lambda).\]
\end{corollary}{}

Lusztig shows the following formula to calculate the stalks of the intersection complexes.

\begin{theorem}[thm:IH_kostka]{\cite{lusztig1981green}}
    The Poincar\'e polynomial of the stalks of $IC_{\overline{\OO_\lambda}}$ at $\OO_\mu$ is
    \[
        \sum_{i}^{}q^i\dim \mathcal{H}^i(IC_{\overline{\OO_\lambda}})_x
        =q^{n(\mu)-n(\lambda)}K_{\lambda,\mu}(q^{-1}),
    \]
    where $n(\lambda):=\sum_{i}^{}(i-1)\lambda_i$ is the dimension of $B_\lambda$ and $x\in\OO_\mu$. In particular,
    \[
        Q(\OO_\lambda,q):=\sum_iq^i\dim \IH(\overline{\OO_\lambda}) = q^{\binom{n}{2}-n(\lambda)}K_{\lambda,(1^n)}(q^{-1}).
    \]
\end{theorem}
